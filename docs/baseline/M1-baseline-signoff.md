## Known limitations of this baseline

Recorded deliberately. Honesty about limitations is worth more than an unsupported claim of completeness.

1. No live CivicConnect stakeholder exists — all needs are derived from the scenario with the team as proxy (AS-01, RSK-10). Requirements are testable; their business validity is not independently confirmed.
2. Target resolution times underlying FR-021 are provisional (RSK-05). The requirement is testable at boundary values; the values are not authorised.
3. Every NFR states a metric and a verification method, but none has been measured. They are commitments, not evidence, until M3.
4. Task-level scheduling and effort estimation are not included; they follow the M2 architecture decision.

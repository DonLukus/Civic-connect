# Team Working Agreement

Agreed by Masego, Don and Emile. Controlled artefact — changes require a pull request.
Reference: DEC-010.

## Workstream ownership

| Member | Workstream |
|---|---|
| Masego | A — problem, stakeholders, scope, constraints, decisions |
| Don | B — requirements, acceptance criteria, RTM, forward engineering, repository setup and governance |
| Emile | C — risk, working agreement, AI control, document control, PED assembly, baseline |

Ownership is drafting responsibility, not exclusive knowledge.

## Cadence

- **Review turnaround: 24 hours.** A fixed daily review window is held so review is never the thing that waits.
- **Change size:** small and single-purpose. If it cannot be reviewed in fifteen minutes, split it.
- **Approval:** two, from members other than the author. No exceptions, no bypass.

## Absence (RSK-04)

With three members, two approvals means unanimity — a single absence blocks every controlled merge.

- Planned absence: announce 24 hours ahead and clear outstanding reviews first.
- Unplanned absence beyond 48 hours: escalate to the lecturer with evidence. **Do not weaken the control.**

## Cross-review

Each member reviews work in both other workstreams. No member reviews only their own area — that
guarantees the knowledge concentration recorded as RSK-13.

## Individual evidence — each member must have

- Meaningful commits in their own name
- Issues owned in their own name
- At least one pull request authored
- At least two pull requests meaningfully reviewed
- The ability to trace one requirement end to end
- The ability to explain one decision **that they did not make**
- The ability to explain one AI-assisted contribution and how it was verified

## Disagreement

Resolved by evidence. Where evidence is unavailable, the decision is deferred and recorded as
deferred with the evidence required — the same standard applied to DEC-007 and DEC-008.
